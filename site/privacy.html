Privacy policy

Family Screen Time Android app (org.familyscreentime). Last updated: 22 October 2026.

In short: Family Screen Time collects no data. Everything the app records stays on the device it is installed on. Nothing is sent to the publisher or to third parties: no account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party services.

Who we are

Family Screen Time is free software (MIT licence) published at familyscreentime.org. Questions: contact@familyscreentime.org.

What the app processes, on the device only

DataWhy
The app on screen and how long it is usedTo count screen time and apply the limits the parent set.
The app usage history Android keeps (about 10 days, on first launch)To show usage statistics from the first day. It is not used for limits.
Rules, app groups, names given to apps, periods, extra time and blocksTo run the app as the parent set it up.
The child's first nameTo show it in the app.
The parent code, stored as a hash (PBKDF2), never in clearTo protect the settings.
The limits the child sets for themselves and their historyTo apply them, and show the parent how they are kept. The child is told.

This data is stored in the app's private storage and erased when the app is uninstalled.

The accessibility service

The app uses Android's accessibility service to know which app is on screen, to close a blocked app, and to show warnings, the countdown and the lock screen over other apps. When the parent turns on uninstall protection, it also closes the settings pages that would switch the app off. To recognise them, it reads screen content only on Android's security and permission pages, and stores nothing. The user is informed and agrees before this service is turned on.

Permissions

  • Accessibility: see above.
  • Usage access: usage history; knowing the app on screen when accessibility is off.
  • Display over other apps: block screen, countdown.
  • Notifications: countdown, warnings, parent mode.
  • Device administrator (optional): preventing uninstallation without the parent. No other administration policy is used.
  • Start at boot, foreground service, exemption from battery optimisation: keeping monitoring running.

Sharing

No data is shared, sold or transferred. The app contains no tracking tools.

Children

The app is designed to be installed by a parent on their child's device. It collects no personal data about the child for the publisher. The child sees their rules and time left, and knows that the parent sees the history of their own limits.

Changes

A future version may offer synchronisation between the family's devices, over the local network or through a server the family hosts itself. This policy will be updated before any version that takes data off the device.

Your rights

As no data leaves the device, the publisher holds no data about you. You can erase all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android's settings. Questions: contact@familyscreentime.org.
