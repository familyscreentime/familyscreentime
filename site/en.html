Family Screen Time
FRNLEN

Screen time, sorted out as a family

For Android today, Windows next. No account, no ads, and everything stays at home.

  • Time per day and per week, for total screen time, a group of apps (games, video…) or a single app.
  • Hours set day by day, mandatory breaks, special periods (holidays, exams…).
  • Grace time, saving unused time, a firm limit after repeated overruns.
  • Quick actions for the parent: extra time, blocking, lifting restrictions for the day, opening hours early.
  • A floating Time Timer-style countdown, warnings, a clear message when time is up.
  • The child sees everything: time left, the rules, and can set limits for themselves.
  • Listen only for music apps: once the time for clips is up, the app opens just long enough to pick the music, which keeps playing in the background.
  • Statistics per app and per device, analysis over several months, export as CSV or for ActivityWatch.
  • Split screen and video in a small window are taken into account: every app on screen counts, and closes when its time is up.
  • Linked devices, if you wish: a child's tablet and phone share their rules and time, and the parent sets everything from their own phone. Each child's device only receives what concerns that child. Directly between your devices over the home Wi-Fi, and away from home through a server you host yourself, always end-to-end encrypted. A device set up on its own can be linked later, without starting over.

The relay server

Optional, so that linked devices also sync away from home. Ordinary web hosting is enough (PHP 8.2 and MySQL, MariaDB or SQLite), with no SSH. It cannot read what it passes on. One server can host other families too, by invitation.
