Privacy policy

Family Screen Time Android app (org.familyscreentime). Last updated: 23 October 2026.

In short: Family Screen Time collects no data. Everything the app records stays on the device it is installed on. Nothing is sent to the publisher or to third parties: no account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party services. If the parent links several devices, they exchange this data with each other, directly over the home Wi-Fi network, end-to-end encrypted.

Who we are

Family Screen Time is free software (MIT licence) published at familyscreentime.org. Questions: contact@familyscreentime.org.

What the app processes, on your devices only

DataWhy
The app on screen and how long it is usedTo count screen time and apply the limits the parent set.
The app usage history Android keeps (about 10 days, on first launch)To show usage statistics from the first day. It is not used for limits.
Rules, each child's app groups, names given to apps, periods, extra time and blocksTo run the app as the parent set it up.
The child's first nameTo show it in the app.
The parent code, stored as a hash (PBKDF2), never in clear; the linked devices' key, encrypted with that code (Argon2id)To protect the settings, and to sign the parent's decisions for the other linked devices.
The limits the child sets for themselves and their historyTo apply them, and show the parent how they are kept. The child is told.

This data is stored in the app's private storage and erased when the app is uninstalled.

Linked devices

This is optional: a device set up on its own talks to no other device. The parent can link devices (a child's tablet and phone, a parent's phone, for example), in parent mode and by comparing a code shown on both screens. They then share the settings, the time used, the list of linked devices (name, role, child) and, for each child's device, the names and icons of the apps installed, so the parent can limit them from another device. This happens directly between the devices, on the home's local network: end-to-end encrypted, and each device checks that the parent's decisions are signed with the linked devices' key. No server of the publisher is involved. A device unlinked or removed is no longer synced; an unlinked device keeps only its child's data and the time used on it.

The accessibility service

The app uses Android's accessibility service to know which app is on screen, to close a blocked app, and to show warnings, the countdown and the lock screen over other apps. When the parent turns on uninstall protection, it also closes the settings pages that would switch the app off. To recognise them, it reads screen content only on Android's security and permission pages, and stores nothing. The user is informed and agrees before this service is turned on.

Permissions

  • Accessibility: see above.
  • Usage access: usage history; knowing the app on screen when accessibility is off.
  • Display over other apps: block screen, countdown.
  • Notifications: countdown, warnings, parent mode.
  • Network (internet, network state, Wi-Fi multicast): find the linked devices on the home network and exchange with them.
  • Device administrator (optional): preventing uninstallation without the parent. No other administration policy is used.
  • Start at boot, foreground service, exemption from battery optimisation: keeping monitoring running.

Sharing

No data is shared with the publisher or third parties, sold or transferred, except between the devices the parent has linked. The app contains no tracking tools.

Children

The app is designed to be installed by a parent on their child's device. It collects no personal data about the child for the publisher. The child sees their rules and time left, and knows that the parent sees the history of their own limits.

Changes

A future version may also sync linked devices through a server the family hosts itself. This policy will be updated before any version that sends data anywhere other than the linked devices.

Your rights

As no data leaves your devices, the publisher holds no data about you. You can erase all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android's settings. Questions: contact@familyscreentime.org.
